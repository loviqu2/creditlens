# Standard Adverse Action Reason Codes (CFPB Form C-1 / C-3)

These are the standardized reason categories used industry-wide on adverse
action notices, as provided in Regulation B's official sample forms.

## Income & Employment
- Income insufficient for amount of credit requested
- Excessive obligations in relation to income (high debt-to-income ratio)
- Length of employment insufficient to qualify
- Unable to verify income or employment

## Credit History
- Insufficient number of credit references provided
- Insufficient credit experience / no credit file
- Poor credit performance with this creditor or others
- Delinquent past or present credit obligations
- Collection action, judgment, garnishment, foreclosure, or bankruptcy
- Number of recent inquiries on credit bureau report

## Credit Scoring Specific
- Insufficient bank references
- Type of occupation
- Insufficient credit experience
- Number of recent inquiries on credit bureau report

## Collateral / Loan Terms
- Value or type of collateral not sufficient
- Loan amount requested relative to collateral or income

Source: 12 CFR Part 1002, Appendix C, Forms C-1 and C-3
