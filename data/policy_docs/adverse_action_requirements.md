# ECOA / Regulation B — Adverse Action Notice Requirements

Under the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) and Regulation B (12 CFR 1002.9),
creditors must provide applicants with specific reasons when adverse action
(such as credit denial) is taken.

## Key Requirements

- The statement of reasons must be specific and indicate the principal
  reason(s) for the adverse action.
- Statements that adverse action was based on "internal standards or
  policies" or that the applicant "failed to achieve a qualifying score" are
  NOT sufficient on their own — actual reasons must be disclosed.
- Disclosure of more than four reasons is not likely to be helpful to the
  applicant; typically 1-4 principal reasons are given.
- The specific reasons disclosed must relate to and accurately describe the
  factors actually considered or scored by the creditor.
- A creditor need not describe how or why a factor adversely affected an
  applicant (e.g., "length of residence" is sufficient, without explaining
  why a short residence period is negative).

## Credit Scoring Systems Specifically

If a creditor bases the denial on a credit scoring system, the reasons
disclosed must relate only to the factors actually scored in the system.
No factor that was a principal reason for adverse action may be excluded
from disclosure — the creditor must disclose the actual reasons for denial
even if the relationship to creditworthiness may not be obvious to the
applicant.

Source: 12 CFR 1002.9, Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
